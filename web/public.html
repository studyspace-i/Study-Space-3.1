About Study Space

One Dashboard to Manage Your Whole Study Space

Study Space is a management system built for study centers and libraries. Instead of tracking seats on paper, attendance in a register, and student details in a notebook, you get one clean dashboard to manage your entire study space — seats, shifts, students, attendance and study logs — from any device. Register once, set up your space in minutes, and let Study Space handle the day-to-day tracking for you.

        FAQ

        Frequently Asked Questions

        Is Study Space free for students?

        Yes. Creating and using a student account on Study Space is completely free. Any fees charged for a seat or membership are set and collected independently by the study center you join, not by Study Space.

        How do I register my study center or library?

        Click "Create a Study Center," enter your center's name, your details and email, verify with the OTP we send you, then pin your location. Your dashboard opens instantly with 100 seats and 5 shifts ready to customise.

        Does Study Space collect fees or payments on my behalf?

        No, not by default. Study Space is a management and discovery tool — fee collection between a study center and its students happens outside the platform, directly between the two parties. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.

        Is my data safe on Study Space?

        We use OTP-verified sign-up, hashed passwords, and role-based access controls to protect accounts. Read our full Privacy Policy for details on what data we collect and how it's used — linked in the footer below.

        Can I add staff members to manage my center?

        Yes. Center owners can create staff/sub-admin accounts with controlled permissions from their admin settings, so your team can help manage seats, students and attendance.
